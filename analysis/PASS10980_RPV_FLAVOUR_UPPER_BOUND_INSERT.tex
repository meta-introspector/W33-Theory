\subsection{Pass 10980: flavour structure does not hide the regenerated $R$-parity violation}

% Follows Pass 10969 (Higgs-quartic ceiling on superpartner masses).
At the Higgs-quartic ceiling $\tilde m\approx3\times10^{10}$\,GeV the proton needs $|\lambda'_{1jk}\lambda''_{11k}|\lesssim10^{-10}$.
In the $23$ $D$-flat $\mathbb{Z}_6$-I vacua, with gauge and space-group rules (which over-permit, so every coupling is an upper
bound), first-generation projections through the up-Yukawa give $2\times10^{-3}$--$0.34$; no suppression mechanism appears,
and the vacua fail independently of proton decay: $7$ have no light $H_u$ and $16$ keep two exotic $d$-triplets massless.
