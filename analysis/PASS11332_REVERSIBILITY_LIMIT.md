**Correction.** Codex's audit (PASS11303_11307, "Parallel intake control") found that Pass 11312's sentence "every long
enough magic circuit breaks the substrate's time reversal" is **false**.
* T⁹ = I, so T^{9m} (with identity Clifford insertions) is reversible at every depth 9m. Checked here for m = 1, 2, 3.
* Measure zero alone gives neither convergence nor a rate.

**Theorem.** For the uniform Clifford+T random word U_k = C_k T ⋯ C₁ T of Pass 11312 (one qutrit),
P(U_k is substrate-time-reversible) → 0.

**Proof.**
1. **The reversible set F is closed and Haar-null.** F is the union, over the finitely many anti-unitary Clifford
   reversals V·K, of the sets {U : V U* V† U scalar}. Each is a real-algebraic subset of the connected manifold PU(3),
   and a proper one because violators exist. A proper real-algebraic subset of a connected analytic manifold has
   measure zero.
2. **The walk equidistributes (Kawada–Itô).** The support Cl·T generates a closed subgroup that contains
   Cl = ⟨(CT)(C′T)⁻¹⟩ and T, hence all of PU(3), since qutrit Clifford+T is universal. PU(3) is a compact simple Lie
   group with no proper nontrivial closed normal subgroup, so the support lies in no coset of one. Hence μ^{*k} → Haar
   weakly.
3. **Portmanteau for the closed set F:** limsup P(U_k ∈ F) ≤ Haar(F) = 0. ∎

**Checks.**

| check | result |
|---|---|
| T⁹ = I and T^{9m} reversible | yes (m = 1, 2, 3) |
| Haar-random U ∈ U(3) reversible (exact criterion) | **0/2000** |
| dimension of the Lie algebra generated by logs of random Clifford+T words | **8** = dim su(3) |

**Not claimed.**
* A rate. The walk operator P_Cl R_T (average over the finite Clifford group, then translate by T) has norm 1 on L².
  An exponential rate would need a spectral gap for the associated Hecke-type operator on Cl\PU(3).
* The 0.72-per-gate decay in Pass 11312 stays an empirical fit.
