\subsection{Pass 11024: the $\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_3$ parity vacua are supersymmetric, and carry massless fractional charges}

% Settles the door Pass 10979 left; corrects its framing ("would need a twelfth-order coupling"): SUSY vacua exist.
On each of the $28$ Pass~10979 vacua the monomials invariant under the continuous charges form a free monoid on $k\in\{1,2\}$
composites $p_i$ (disjoint products of $3$ or $4$ fields, lattice index $1$), and $p^a$ is allowed in $W$ iff
$a_1+\dots+a_k\equiv1\pmod3$, checked on a full period, hence to all orders: $W|_S=F(p)$ with $F(\omega p)=\omega F(p)$.
With every field nonzero $F_\phi=0\iff\nabla_pF=0$, which has the B\'ezout number of roots; every root lifts to an exact
$F=D=0$ point with the FI term cancelled and every field nonzero ($28/28$), at $\max|\phi|\approx0.8$--$1.07\,M_s$ for $O(1)$
couplings.  A hidden-representation-refined index then shows $48$ chiral states of charge $\pm\tfrac13$, six of them
hidden singlets, massless in every vacuum preserving the hidden $SU(4)\times SU(3)'$; both are infrared free
($b=-7,-16$ and $-4,-13$), and no single $SU(4)$ meson condensate added to any vacuum pairs the fractional class.
Breaking one factor at a time shows the index vanishes iff the hidden $SU(4)$ is broken; a Luty--Taylor census of every
FI-cancelling ray containing a quadratic $SU(4)$ condensate finds $54$ parity vacua ($39+15$), all supersymmetric with
aligned parity, and in every one at least $120$ fractional multiplets stay massless at all orders, most of them
protected by an unbroken discrete symmetry, a $3$-group of order $3^4$ or $3^6$ (the torsion of the charge lattice modulo the vacuum's).
An exact lattice solver (Smith normal form, LLL, congruence-free residual program, rational re-verification) reproduces
every integer-program result of Passes 10978--10980 with $0$ differences.
