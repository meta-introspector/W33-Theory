**Data.** P₁ … P₁₀, exact (Passes 11312, 11422, 11436, 11488, 11500, 11513). In integers,
a_k = P_k·216·8^{k−1}/18 = 11, 57, 347, 1869, 11003, 60981, 353927, 1995825, 11519231, 65621121.

## Results

* **No C-finite law of low order.**
  * No linear recurrence with constant coefficients of order ≤ 4, homogeneous or with a constant term, fits the ten
    terms.
  * None of order ≤ 2 fits either parity subsequence.
  * So the depth sequence is not generated by a small transfer matrix acting on the walk, at least not one of size ≤ 4.
* **The local rate is still rising.**
  * Two-step rates √(P_{k+2}/P_k) from odd k: 0.7021, 0.7039, 0.7089, 0.7131.
  * From even k: 0.7158, 0.7140, 0.7151, 0.7168.
  * Both chains end increasing.
  * They remain below the ≈ 0.72 sampled with the tight cut at depths 12–30 (Pass 11459) and below
    ρ₍₈,₈₎ = 0.72276 (Pass 11372).
* **Reading.** The approach to ≈ 0.72 is slow. Exact data to depth 10 cannot decide whether the limit is ρ₍₈,₈₎, another
  constant, or 1. No limiting value is claimed.

**Why depth 11 was not computed.** It would need about 1.9·10⁸ distinct depth-9 operators, 1.4·10¹⁰ prefilter evaluations.
That is out of reach of this machine in one session. Pass 11513's method would scale to it with disk-backed hashing.
