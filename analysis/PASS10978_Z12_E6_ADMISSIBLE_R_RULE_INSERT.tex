\subsection{Pass 10978: the E6 lattice fixes the admissible $R$-rule of $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I}

% Resolves the Pass 10974 'undetermined' Z12-I verdicts; rule origin: Bizet et al. arXiv:1301.2322.
The $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I twist on the $E_6$ lattice is the Coxeter element $c$.  Generating $W(E_6)$ ($51\,840$ elements)
exactly gives $C_W(c)=\langle c\rangle$ and $-1\notin W(E_6)$, so the lattice symmetries commuting with the twist are
$\langle c\rangle\times\langle-1\rangle$, and $-c^6$ is a $\pi$-rotation of the order-$3$ plane only: the single admissible
$R$-symmetry is $\mathbb{Z}_2^R$ on that plane.  Bracketing every verdict between the weakest rules and this one,
all $14$ parity-viable $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I vacua are decided: $11$ keep exotic $d$-triplets massless (non-negative
monomials, all orders), one has no light Higgs, one fails at both ends, and $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$I$_{1063}$ --- exotics massive,
one Higgs pair protected at all orders --- has an outside singlet entering linearly at order $4$, and absorbing any
low-order linear singlet lifts the protected pair.  The Pass 10967 statement on $u^cd^cd^c$ in $\mathbb{Z}_6$II$_{23}$
survives every rule set.
