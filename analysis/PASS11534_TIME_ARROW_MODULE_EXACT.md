## Exact method

* **Exact evaluation.** At points ψ ∈ ℤ[ω]³ every stabiliser probability is rational: q_s = 3|⟨s|ψ⟩|² is an integer. So
  every invariant R_m = (1/432) Σ_g sgn^ε(g) Π q_{g m_i} / 3^{deg} is rational. Everything is computed modulo the prime
  P = 2 147 483 629, which divides none of the denominators, at 240 points.
* **Why rank mod P proves generation.** For an integer matrix, rank mod P ≤ rank over ℚ. The rank over ℚ of the
  evaluation matrix of degree-k products is at most the dimension of their span, which is at most the Molien dimension
  E_k (or O_k) of Pass 11491.
  * So **rank mod P = E_k (or O_k) proves that the products span the whole degree**.
  * The number of linear relations among the listed products is then exactly #products − E_k (or O_k).
* **When a new generator is proved.** It is proved by counting when there are fewer distinct products of lower generators
  than the dimension.

## Results (all degrees 1 … 20)

**The time-even ring.** It is generated by **the stabiliser moments**

M_k(ψ) = Σ_s |⟨s|ψ⟩|^{2k},  k = 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9,

one in each of those degrees. The search picks R(p_{Z0}^k) = M_k/12, the first candidate in every degree.
* M₂ is absent because Σ_s p_s² is the 2-design frame potential, a multiple of |ψ|⁴.
* Each of the eight is **proved necessary by counting**.
* They generate every E_k, k ≤ 20, with no further generator.
* **Relations among their products** first appear in degree 14: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 11, 16 in degrees 14 … 20.

**The time-odd module (the arrows).** It is generated over that ring by **the five MUB chiralities**

g_{k+3} = Σ_g sgn(g) p_{ga}^k p_{gb}² p_{gc},  k = 3 … 7  (Pass 11515).

* Each is **proved necessary by counting**.
* They generate every O_k, k ≤ 20, with no further generator.

| degree | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| relations among odd products | 1 | 3 | 6 | 10 | 16 | 24 | 35 | 50 |

> **Theorem (through degree 20).** Every Clifford-invariant polynomial of a qutrit state of degree ≤ 20 is a polynomial
> in the stabiliser moments M₁, M₃, …, M₉ plus a combination, with such polynomial coefficients, of the five chiralities
> g₆ … g₁₀. The time-symmetric part is the polynomial, and the time arrows are the chirality part.

**A cautionary near-miss (not claimed).**
* Through degree 20 the even relation counts 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 11, 16 equal O_{k−8} exactly.
* From the Molien series, the same count would be 23 against O₁₃ = 22 at degree 21, and the two keep diverging.
* So "even relations = t⁸·O(t)" is a coincidence of the computed range.

**Scope.** Generation is proved through degree 20, not beyond. The degree bound for these invariants (finite group times
U(1)) is not established.

counts and the chiralities). Exact rational evaluation of stabiliser probabilities is new here.
