\subsection*{One fixed W33 perfect gate compiles every two-qutrit Clifford}

Let $H=(\mathrm{SL}(2,3)\times\mathrm{SL}(2,3)){:}C_2$ be the order-$1152$
stabilizer of one of the $45$ W33 tensor factorizations, and let $p$ be the
order-four symplectic tetracode perfect gate. Exact double-coset enumeration
and objectwise word replay give
\[
 \Sp(4,3)=H\;\sqcup\;HpH\;\sqcup\;Hp h_*pH
\]
for one fixed $h_*\in H$. The three pieces have sizes
$1152,13824,36864$ and optimal $p$-depths $0,1,2$ respectively. In
particular, every two-qutrit Clifford uses at most two copies of the same
perfect interaction, and the standard qutrit SUM gate has a frozen exact word
$Lph_*pR$ of minimal depth two.

On the $45$ factorizations, if $A$ is the perfect-relation adjacency matrix,
\[
 A^2=12I+3A+3(J-I-A),\qquad
 \operatorname{Spec}(A)=\{12^1,3^{20},(-3)^{24}\}.
\]
This rank-three Hecke identity is the compiler algebra: graph diameter two is
the depth bound. The fixed gate remains Clifford. Approximate universality
uses the separate signed-$E_6$ cubic zero-controlled-$X$ resource already
certified in Pass 10944; no physical implementation is claimed.

The same walk is exactly lumpable on the Pass 11185 decomposition of the
$45$ cubic monomials into $5$ terms of type $1\cdot10\cdot10$ and $40$ of
type $16\cdot16\cdot10$.  Its neighbour quotient and stochastic form are
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}4&8\\1&11\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 P=\begin{pmatrix}1/3&2/3\\1/12&11/12\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The stationary weights are $(1/9,8/9)$ and the transient mode decays by
$1/4$ per perfect step.  This is an exact dynamics on Yukawa-term labels,
not a computation of Yukawa amplitudes, masses or mixing angles.
The tetracode carrier itself is prior (Passes 10946, 10954--10955, 10970,
11035 and 11164); the increment here is the optimal two-qutrit word theorem
and its exact $E_6$ sector quotient.
