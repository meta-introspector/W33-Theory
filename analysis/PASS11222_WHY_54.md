# Pass 11222: why χ = 54·Q (partial answer)

**Question.** Pass 11212 found that, on every member of the 256 pair, the profile-oriented Maslov count χ
(Pass 11209) equals 54 × the twist Q (master's Pass 11189). Where does 54 come from?

**Method.**
* For a relation (F₀, F) let Γ be the subgroup of the local Cliffords and qutrit relabellings that fixes both splits.
* Γ acts on oriented triples (L₁, L₂, M) of product Lagrangians: L₁ and L₂ from F₀ with profile(L₁) > profile(L₂), and
  M from F.
* Γ preserves both the profile orientation and the Kashiwara–Maslov class. So χ is a signed sum of Γ-orbit sizes,
  +1 for an odd-class orbit of class 1 and −1 for one of class 3.

**Results (exact).**

| relation | \|Γ\| | odd-triple orbit sizes | unbalanced orbits | χ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256 pair, Q = −1 | 324 = 2²·3⁴ | 27, 54, 81, 162 (409 orbits) | −1 of size 162, +1 of size 81, +1 of size 27 | −54 |
| 256 pair, Q = +1 | 324 | same | mirror image | +54 |
| 6912 pair | 12 | 1, 2, 3, 6 | net ±2 orbits of size 3, ±2 of size 6 | ±18 |

* **27 divides χ on the 256 pair.** Every odd-class orbit has size divisible by 27, because Γ's Sylow 3-subgroup
  (order 81) fixes no odd triple except through a subgroup of order 3. The 6912 pair has |Γ| = 12, so nothing forces a
  factor 27 there, and indeed χ = ±18.
* **54 = 2·27.** Classes 1 and 3 balance orbit-size by orbit-size in all but three orbits. The net, in units of 27, is
  −6 + 3 + 1 = −2 for Q = −1.
* **Where the chirality lives.** Only four (profile, intersection) cells carry net chirality, with nets ±162, ∓54,
  ∓243 and ±81. In all four, L₂ and M have the "unique-partner" profile (45, 18, 1, 0): each meets exactly one product
  Lagrangian of the other split in dimension 2.

**What is not explained.**
* Why exactly three orbits are unbalanced, with sizes 162, 81 and 27, so that the net is 2 rather than another
  multiple of 27, is not derived.
* χ is quadratic in the gate: a trace tr(P_{L₁} P_{L₂} U P_{M₀} U†). The Gauss sum of Pass 11201 is a third moment. So
  their proportionality on the 256 pair links invariants of different degree. A degree-raising identity would be the
  natural proof, and it is left open.
