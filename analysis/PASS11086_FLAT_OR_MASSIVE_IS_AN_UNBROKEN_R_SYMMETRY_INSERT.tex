\subsection{Pass 11076: ``flat-or-massive'' is an unbroken $R$-symmetry, not a law}

% Corrects the causal reading "the exotic triplet is massless because W vanishes on the vacuum".
With the exact lattice solver, every parity vacuum with a decided exotic spectrum is classified by whether $W|_S$ is
forbidden by an exact unbroken symmetry of the vacuum (integer exponents of any sign fail) or only by holomorphy, and
likewise for the exotic $d\bar d$ masses.  Where both vanish ($\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_3$ minimal supports $36/36$,
$\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I under $\mathbb{Z}_2^R$ $12/14$), $W$ is always forbidden by an unbroken discrete $R$-symmetry, which in
$46$ of the $48$ cases also forbids the exotic masses (the other $2$ are holomorphy-only). But neither implies the other:
under $\mathbb{Z}_2^R$ two $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I vacua (one is $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$I$_{1063}$) have $W$ forbidden and the exotics
massive, and under the weakest rules eleven have $W\neq0$ and the exotics massless.
