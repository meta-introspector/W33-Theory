# Pass 11236 — the cubic (magic) gate as a residual flavour symmetry: the no-go survives the magic layer

Producer: `analysis/w33_pass11236_cubic_flavour.py`
Certificate: `data/w33_pass11236_cubic_flavour.json`
Regression: `tests/test_w33_pass11236_11239.py`

**Background.**
* The parallel session's Pass 11225 scanned every residual-symmetry pattern inside the geometry's finite symmetry
  groups, W(E6), Sp(4,3) and all their subgroups. It found no complete PMNS pattern and no Cabibbo angle.
* Single columns survive: TM₁ from the line stabiliser, and an order-9 Clifford column. Pass 11230 shows the latter fits
  only at the upper θ₂₃ edge, with δ ≈ π.
* The cubic gate T = ζ₉^{x³} is not in those groups. It is the non-Clifford resource that breaks substrate T-symmetry
  (Passes 11213, 11227). This pass asks whether it, as a residual symmetry, rescues complete mixing.

**Scan.** One qutrit carries the three generations. For Dirac neutrinos, any abelian residual group with
non-degenerate spectrum fixes U_ν. U = U_e†U_ν is then compared, under all row and column permutations, with
NuFIT 6.0 at 3σ (normal ordering, as quoted in Pass 11225) and with |V_us|. Pairings tested:
* G_e = ⟨C T C†⟩ for all 216 Cliffords C, against G_ν = ⟨g⟩ for all 182 Cliffords g with non-degenerate spectrum;
* G_e or G_ν (or both) generated by finite-order **one-cubic-gate words** C₁ T C₂. These have orders 3, 6 and 9, and give
  148 distinct eigenbases that are **not** stabilizer bases.

**Result: no complete PMNS pattern and no Cabibbo pattern in any pairing.**

| pairing | modulus patterns | PMNS complete | Cabibbo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ⟨CTC†⟩ × Clifford | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| one-cubic-gate word × Clifford | 44 | 0 | 0 |
| Clifford × one-cubic-gate word | 44 | 0 | 0 |
| one-cubic-gate word × one-cubic-gate word | 41 | 0 | 0 |

* **Why ⟨CTC†⟩ adds nothing.** T is diagonal in the Z eigenbasis, which is a stabilizer basis. So ⟨CTC†⟩ gives the same
  U_e as the Clifford ⟨CZC†⟩, and only the 6 Clifford modulus patterns appear. As a residual symmetry the cubic gate's
  new content lies in its eigenvalues (phases), not its eigenvectors.
* **Genuine magic eigenbases do not help either.** The 148 non-stabilizer eigenbases from one-cubic-gate words produce
  41–44 new modulus patterns, none inside the 3σ box.

**Reading.** The Pass 11225 no-go on complete mixing from the geometry's symmetry extends to the magic layer at
one-cubic-gate depth. The cubic resource can supply CP-odd phases (Passes 11213, 11227) but, as a residual symmetry, not
the angles. Mixing angles still need dynamics (vacuum alignment, Pass 11230).

**Scope.** Dirac case, one qutrit as generation space, words with one cubic gate. Single surviving columns from the new
magic patterns, and the Majorana (Klein-group) case with magic elements, are not scanned here.
