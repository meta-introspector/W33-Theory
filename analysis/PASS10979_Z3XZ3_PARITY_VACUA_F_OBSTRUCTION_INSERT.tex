\subsection{Pass 10979: the $W(3,3)$ twist on the prime orbifolds $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_3$ and $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_3$}

% Every plane prime => every R-rule established (Bizet et al. arXiv:1301.2322).  Z3xZ3 MSSM-like models are known
% (arXiv:1808.06622); new here is the W(3,3)-twisted sample and its parity/F-flatness analysis.
With the unique $A_8$ modular-invariant Kac pair, $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_3$ gives no Standard Model in $64\,000$ Wilson-line draws,
corroborating the cyclic order-three law; $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_3$ gives $13$ ($12$ distinct spectra).  All planes are
prime, so every $R$-rule is established: parity exists in $4$, and $3$ keep it on FI-cancelling $D$-flat rays --- the first
such family.  On all $36$ MSSM-viable minimal supports the exotic $d$-triplet is massless to all orders and
$W|_S\equiv0$, while outside singlets enter $W$ linearly at order $3$--$4$; absorbing them gives $28$ vacua with joint
MSSM-viable parity, massive exotics and full-support FI-cancelling $D$-flatness (Higgs massless to all orders in $22$).
$F$-flatness with every field nonzero needs $\sum_k(c_km_k)e_k=0$, i.e.\ linearly dependent exponent vectors: enumerating
every allowed monomial to degree $14$, they are independent through degree $11$ in all $28$ (through $14$ in $12$).  No
supersymmetric vacuum exists through degree $11$ for any couplings; the first balance, cubic against degree $12$, needs
$c_{12}/c_3\sim(M_s/\langle\phi\rangle)^9$ --- a worldsheet-instanton question left open.
